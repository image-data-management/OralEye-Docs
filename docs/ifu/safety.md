# Safety Information

## Important Safety Instructions

:::danger Critical Safety Information
Read and understand all safety information before using the OralEye system. Failure to follow these instructions may result in injury or device damage.
:::

## General Warnings

### ⚠️ Electrical Safety

- Use only the provided power adapter
- Do not expose the device to liquids
- Disconnect power before cleaning
- Do not use if power cord is damaged

### ⚠️ Patient Safety

- Ensure patient comfort during imaging procedures
- Avoid prolonged LED exposure to eyes
- Use appropriate infection control procedures
- Follow standard precautions for patient contact

### ⚠️ Optical Safety

- LED light source is safe for intended use
- Do not stare directly into LED array
- Automatic exposure control prevents overexposure

## Infection Control

### Required Procedures

1. **Barrier Protection:** Use disposable barriers for intraoral components
2. **Surface Disinfection:** Clean all surfaces with approved disinfectant after each patient
3. **Hand Hygiene:** Follow standard hand hygiene protocols

### Approved Disinfectants

- Isopropyl alcohol (70%)
- EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants
- Follow manufacturer's contact time recommendations

:::warning
Do not autoclave or immerse device in liquid. Do not use abrasive cleaners.
:::

## Environmental Conditions

### Operating Environment

- **Temperature:** 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F)
- **Humidity:** 30% to 75% relative humidity
- **Pressure:** 700 hPa to 1060 hPa

### Storage Environment

- **Temperature:** -10°C to 50°C (14°F to 122°F)
- **Humidity:** 10% to 90% relative humidity (non-condensing)

## Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC)

The OralEye system complies with IEC 60601-1-2 electromagnetic compatibility standards.

### Precautions

- Keep away from strong electromagnetic fields
- Do not use near MRI equipment
- Maintain minimum distances from RF communication equipment

## Disposal

### End of Life

- Do not dispose of device in regular waste
- Follow local regulations for electronic waste disposal
- Contact manufacturer for recycling options

### Battery Disposal

- Lithium-ion batteries must be recycled according to local regulations
- Contact local waste management for battery recycling

## Emergency Procedures

### Device Malfunction

1. Immediately stop using the device
2. Disconnect power if safe to do so
3. Contact technical support
4. Do not attempt repair

### Patient Adverse Event

1. Stop procedure immediately
2. Provide appropriate medical care
3. Report incident to manufacturer
4. Report to regulatory authorities as required

## Reporting

Report adverse events or device problems to:

- **Manufacturer:** support@oraleye.ai
- **FDA:** via MedWatch (1-800-FDA-1088)
